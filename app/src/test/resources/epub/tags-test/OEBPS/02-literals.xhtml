02. Literal asterisks and underscores

The author's punctuation must reach the reader unchanged.

2.1. Asterisk footnote: (*) — the asterisk in parentheses must be visible.

2.2. Numbered footnote: a month passed after the event (*1).

2.3. Two numbered ones in one paragraph: footnotes (*1) and (*2) — both intact.

2.4. Underscores in a URL: https://example.org/wiki/Some_Long_Title_With_Underscores

2.5. Underscores inside a word: snake_case_identifier

2.6. A lone underscore in parentheses: (_)

2.7. Private-use characters the book brought itself, from the block the parser once used for its own marks:  — and the Apple logo, U+F8FF: . They must NOT switch on a style. As of #29 the first ones are shown as they are, like any other character the book holds.

2.8. Two bare asterisks in parentheses, both surrounded by punctuation, are an emphasis pair to CommonMark: before the DOM walk (#29) the next line lost both and the text between them turned italic. This book is not markdown, so they must stay.
